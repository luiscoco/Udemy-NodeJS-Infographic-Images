Category: JavaScript · Purpose: Present time and numeric values consistently across locales.
Format the same timestamp and currency amount using Intl for Spain and the United States. Specify time zones explicitly and verify examples around a daylight-saving transition without relying on the machine’s default locale.

intl.mjs: const times=['2026-03-29T00:30:00Z','2026-03-29T01:30:00Z']; const fmt=new Intl.DateTimeFormat('en-GB',{timeZone:'Europe/Madrid',hour:'2-digit',minute:'2-digit',hour12:false}); console.log(times.map(t=>fmt.format(new Date(t)))); console.log(new Intl.NumberFormat('es-ES',{style:'currency',currency:'EUR'}).format(1234.5)); console.log(new Intl.NumberFormat('en-US',{style:'currency',currency:'USD'}).format(1234.5)); Expected times 01:30 → 03:30 spring forward; localized currency spacing/punctuation may vary with ICU. Always name time zone and locale. Spain vs US currency formatting not exchange-rate conversion. node intl.mjs.
